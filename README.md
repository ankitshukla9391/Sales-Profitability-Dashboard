# 📊 Excel Sales Dashboard

An interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel**
to analyze revenue, profitability, units sold, sales channels, regions,
countries, and monthly performance.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to transform raw sales transaction data
into a clear and interactive dashboard that helps answer questions such
as:

-   How much revenue and gross profit did the business generate?
-   Which months performed best and worst?
-   Which regions and countries contribute the most revenue?
-   Which sales channel is the strongest?
-   Which product categories and products drive sales?
-   How efficiently is revenue being converted into gross profit?

## 🔎 Business Insights

### ⭐ Major Findings

-   **Online Store** is the leading sales channel.
-   **Western Europe** generates the highest regional revenue.
-   **United Kingdom** is the top-performing country by revenue.
-   **Camping** is the highest-performing product category.
-   **June** is the best month by revenue.
-   Overall gross margin is approximately **45.94%**, indicating that a
    substantial portion of revenue remains after COGS.
-   The business sold **6,698 units across 2,707 orders**, giving an
    average revenue per order of approximately **\$238.35**.

### 💡 Possible Business Actions

-   Continue investing in the **Online Store**, since it is the largest
    revenue contributor.
-   Investigate successful strategies in **Western Europe and the UK**
    and consider applying them to lower-performing markets.
-   Focus inventory and marketing on high-performing categories such as
    **Camping and Hiking**.
-   Analyze the lower-revenue months, especially **February**, to
    identify seasonal or promotional opportunities.
-   Monitor discounts and product-level margins to ensure revenue growth
    does not reduce profitability.

### Important Calculations

**Revenue**

`Units Sold × Unit Price × (1 − Discount %)`

**COGS**

`Units Sold × Unit Cost`

**Gross Profit**

`Revenue − COGS`

**Gross Margin**

`Gross Profit ÷ Revenue`
